# Hushfall: story and progression (draft 1)

This is the plan for the story, and for how the story teaches the player to
play. It keeps everything the game already has (Vael, the Hush, the Kith, the
five shrines, the Hollow King) and gives it reasons, people and a shape.

Tags show how much work each idea is:

- **[built]** already in the game
- **[words]** only new dialogue (quick)
- **[tweak]** a small data change, such as which Kith or enemies appear where
- **[new]** needs new code

---

## Decided

Settled on 2026-11-22:

1. **Wren is a foundling of the Lantern Tree.** Maren found Wren as a baby
   among its roots the night the Hush fell. Wren has never heard Vael's song,
   yet the Kith answer Wren as if Wren were part of it. The ending reveals why:
   Wren is the last note of the song, the one the Hollow King could not
   silence.
2. **The Hollow King is freed, not killed.** He remembers what he lost, lays
   down the crown and fades into the returning song.
3. **There is a rival, Sable**, another young Kith-bonder who thinks the Hush
   is a mercy. Sable duels Wren between chapters, then steps aside at the end.
4. **The tone is warm and bittersweet:** a cosy village, sad monsters who were
   once good, a hopeful ending.

Still open (assumed for now): **Wren is a silent hero**, and everyone else
does the talking.

---

## 1. The heart of it

**Pitch:** In a valley where everything once sang, a silence has fallen. A kid
who has never heard the song sets out with creatures who still remember it, to
wake five sleeping shrines and face the grieving king who made the world quiet.

**Theme: bonds are worth the pain of remembering.** The Hollow King silenced
Vael because the song kept reminding him of the friend he lost. Wren wins by
doing the opposite: making bonds, carrying memories, and fighting *with*
others. That is why the game's power comes from Kith bonds (the Bond Rite,
Bond Arts, Unison Burst). The mechanics are the theme.

**What "the Hush" is:** not just quiet. Where the Hush is thick, things forget
who they were. People turn into Husks, keepers turn into monsters, colours
fade. Song is memory; silence is forgetting.

## 2. What happened before the game

- **The Choir of Vael.** Five keepers looked after the valley's song, each in
  a shrine: the Rootwarden (Verdant), the Kiln (Ember), the Cantor (Tide), the
  Stormcaller (Storm), and the Blade of the Kith, who wandered the valley and
  bound it together (Gloam). Elder Maren was their teacher.
- **Aurel**, Maren's best student, became the Blade of the Kith. His partner was
  an Umbrafox named **Lumen**, his closest friend since childhood.
- **The Night of the Long Note** (about sixteen years ago). Something went
  wrong deep under the valley in the **Undersong**, the silence beneath the
  world. Lumen went into the dark to hold it back and did not come out.
- Aurel could not bear it. Every stream and stone sang, and every song
  reminded him of Lumen. He went down into the Undersong and brought back the
  **Hollow Crown**, which makes everything around it forget. When he put it
  on, the Hush fell across Vael.
- The other keepers tried to stop him. The Hush took them one by one, and they
  became the guardians Wren now fights.
- That same night, Maren found a baby among the roots of the Lantern Tree:
  **Wren**.
- **Twist for the ending:** Lumen did not die. Lumen became part of the Gloam,
  and has been trying to reach Aurel ever since. The Umbrafox you glimpse in
  Gloamwood ("You only see it when it wants to be seen") is Lumen.

## 4. The journey, chapter by chapter

Every chapter follows the same rhythm, so players always know where they are:

1. **Arrive**: a new place, a new problem, someone who needs help.
2. **Learn**: one new skill, taught by a person or a safe situation.
3. **Practise**: camps and wild Kith that need the new skill.
4. **Prepare**: hints point to the right Kith and upgrades for the boss.
5. **Test**: the shrine's rooms, then the boss, which examines that skill.
6. **Reward**: a relic that gives a new ability and opens new places.
7. **Turn**: a story beat (a memory, a rival duel) that pulls you onward.

### Prologue: Lanternrest (level 1 to 2)

**Story.** Wren wakes to find the last lit lantern on the Lantern Tree
flickering. Maren says the Hush is creeping closer. Three young Kith have come
to the tree, which has not happened in sixteen years.

**Learn, in order** (each only once the last one is done):

| Step | Who and where | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move, zoom, talk | walk from Wren's house to Maren under the tree | [built] tips exist; [words] give Maren the lines |
| Choose a partner Kith | Maren, the three starters | [built] |
| Light combo, lock-on, dodge | **Brannoc's practice yard**: three straw dummies, then Brannoc swings a stave you must dodge | [built] |
| Heavy attack | Brannoc: "That dummy has a shield. Hit it hard." | [built] |
| Bond Art | first real fight: a lone Husk wanders into the village at the end of the yard | [built] |
| The Bond Rite | **Oona's grove**: a sleepy Kith (a Mossling, or a Voltkit or other common if you have one) to weaken and bond, with Oona talking you through the ring | [built] |
| Waystones and Dewdrops | the Waystone by the village; Pip gives you your first Threads | [built] mostly |

**Turn.** Maren tells Wren about the five shrines. The first is just past the
old stones to the northwest.

### Chapter 1: Lanternrest Meadow, the Shrine of Roots (level 1 to 4)

**Story.** The meadow is greying at the edges. Ghorn, the gentle giant who
tended the Lantern Tree's roots, has walled himself inside his shrine.

- **New skill:** fighting groups (combos, dodging, lock-on switching), and
  your first look at **elements**.
- **How it is taught:** the shrine door reads "*Ember wakes what roots
  forget.*" Oona adds: "Roots fear fire, child." If your starter was not
  Cinderpup, a stray Cinderpup sleeps by Brannoc's forge and can be bonded;
  Brannoc and Oona mention it only if you have no Cinderpup. [built]
- **The test:** Ghorn's huge, slow sweeps and slams are a dodging exam. At half
  health he calls Skitters, which tests crowd control. [built]
- **Reward:** the Heart of Root, a third Kith slot. [built] Oona explains party
  roles: "One to hit hard, one to heal or shield, one for whatever you face
  next." [words]
- **Turn:** freed, Ghorn says only "Tell Maren the Tree remembers her," and the
  first lantern relights. [words] + [new] lantern light. **Sable** waits at the
  east pass: "You woke the Rootwarden? He was *happy* asleep." First duel
  (short, Sable holds back). [new]

### Chapter 2: Emberridge, the Shrine of Cinders (level 6 to 9)

**Story.** Brannoc admits why he left Emberridge: the night the Hush came, he
ran, and left his Kith partner Kiln waiting at the shrine forge. Kiln became
Cindermaw.

- **New skill:** **parrying**. Brutes hit too hard to trade blows with.
- **How it is taught:** the first Brute stands alone on the road from the
  Emberridge Gate Waystone, and a tip about parrying shows when it notices
  you. [built] Before that, Brannoc runs a forge trial: "Parry three of my
  swings." [built]
- **Prepare:** Tide beats Ember. Pip mentions the hot springs a short walk
  south of the Emberridge Gate Waystone, where Puddlefins and Shellgars soak.
  [built]
- **The test:** Cindermaw's charges punish standing still and reward a
  sidestep or parry; its fire breath teaches positioning. [built]
- **Reward:** the Ember Sigil, which unlocks Unison Burst. [built] Story
  framing: as Kiln remembers Brannoc, you feel your own Kith's bond surge.
  [words]
- **Turn:** Brannoc reforges your blade for free and is visibly lighter. The
  rune on the village well cracks: the way to the Abyss opens. [built]

### Chapter 3: Mirrormere, the Shrine of Tides (level 11 to 14)

**Story.** The mere is a perfect mirror with no reflection of the sky. Under it
sleeps Maressa, the Cantor, who drowned her own voice to keep it from the
crown. This is the midpoint: Echo Shards here reveal that Maren trained the
Hollow King.

- **New skill:** **breaking guards**. Warders block everything from the front.
- **How it is taught:** the first Warder stands alone on the way from the
  Mirrormere Shore Waystone, with a tip about heavy attacks and flanking.
  [built] A fisher NPC shouting "Hit it *hard*, or get behind it!" would be a
  nice extra. [new]
- **Prepare:** Storm beats Tide. Voltkit from the meadow is the hint: Maren
  says "Bring something that crackles." [words]
- **The test:** Maressa keeps her distance with rings, walls and volleys, so
  the exam is **closing the gap**: dodge chains and Dash Slash. [built]
- **Reward:** the Tidewalker Pearl, a double jump. [built] New ledges appear in
  the meadow and Emberridge, with Echo Shards on top, so it pays to go back.
  [new] a few ledges
- **Turn:** Maressa sings one verse before fading, the first true song Wren has
  ever heard. The overworld music gains a voice. [new] Sable's second duel:
  Sable is harder now and uses a Gloam Kith. [new]

### Chapter 4: Stormreach, the Shrine of Gales (level 16 to 19)

**Story.** Skarn has not been taken by the Hush at all. He serves the Hollow
King by choice, because Aurel is his friend. He wants to see if Wren is strong
enough to be worth hurting his friend for.

- **New skill:** **perfect dodges and switching targets**. Wraiths blink
  around you.
- **How it is taught:** the first Wraith waits alone on the stair from the
  Stormreach Stair Waystone, with a tip about lock-on and last-instant dodges,
  and Brannoc suggests the Flash Step skill. [built] Skarn's soldiers sparring
  with you on the stair would be a nice extra. [new]
- **Prepare:** Verdant beats Storm. Your meadow Kith are the answer, and Oona
  reminds you they have probably evolved by now. [words]
- **The test:** Skarn blinks and fires beams. It is a reflex exam, closest to
  Ninja Gaiden in feel. [built]
- **Reward:** the Stormstride Feather, so your dodge becomes a lightning strike.
  Mastering defence turns it into attack. [built]
- **Turn:** Skarn, beaten, opens the west pass himself: "He was not always
  like this. Remind him." Sable reaches Gloamwood first. [words] + [new]

### Chapter 5: Gloamwood, the Hollow Court (level 21 to 25)

**Story.** The Hush is loudest here. Everything mixes: every enemy type, elite
packs, and Gloam, which has no weakness and no strength. A black fox keeps
appearing at the edge of the screen.

- **New skill:** none. This chapter tests everything together, and leans on
  your bonds: Kith Arts and Unison Burst.
- **Sable's last duel** happens at the Court gate. Sable loses, and finally
  admits they miss the people they lost. Sable steps aside. [new]
- **The Hollow King, three phases:** [built] phases exist; [new] phase 3 twist
  1. **Swordsman:** Aurel fights with a moveset like Wren's. A parry exam.
  2. **Crown:** Gloam storms and summons. A dodging exam.
  3. **The silence:** the crown silences your Kith. Bond Arts stop working and
     the music cuts out. You fight alone, badly outmatched. Then your Kith sing
     anyway: the Bond gauge fills by itself and the **Unison Burst** button
     lights up. Lumen appears beside Wren and joins in. The burst breaks the
     crown.
- **Ending:** Aurel sees Lumen and remembers. He sets the crown down and fades
  into the returning song with his friend. Wren hears Vael's song for the
  first time, and finally understands what Wren is: the last note of it, kept
  safe in the Lantern Tree. Every lantern lights. [words] + [new] scene

### After the ending

- **The Abyss** is the Undersong, the silence under the world, and it has no
  bottom. Its floor bosses are **echoes** of the keepers (as now). [built]
  [words]
- **Lumen** stays in Gloamwood and can be bonded: the rarest Kith. [new] small
- **Goals:** bond with every Kith, find all 16 Echo Shards (which unlocks a
  last memory of young Maren, Aurel and Lumen), seal every Hush Rift, push your
  Abyss record. [built] mostly

## 5. How the player learns

**Rules for teaching**

1. **One new thing at a time.** Each chapter adds one skill, not three.
2. **A person teaches it, a place practises it, a boss tests it.** Tips can
   stay as reminders, but the first time should come from someone in the
   world.
3. **Each new enemy arrives alone first.** The first Brute, Warder and Wraith
   should each be met on their own, before you see them in a group.
4. **Every relic is a new verb**, and every new verb opens something,
   including places you have already been.
5. **Hints, not walls.** NPCs say which element to bring, and where to find
   it, but a player who ignores them can still win by playing well.

**What is taught where**

| Skill | Introduced | Practised | Tested |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move, jump, talk | prologue | village | |
| Light combo, lock-on, dodge | Brannoc's yard | meadow camps | Ghorn |
| Heavy attack | Brannoc's yard | shielded dummies | Warders |
| Kith partner and Bond Art | Maren, first Husk | every fight | |
| Bond Rite (taming) | Oona's grove | wild Kith in every region | |
| Elements and counters | the Roots door, Oona | choosing your party | every boss |
| Party of three, swapping | Heart of Root | Emberridge | Cindermaw onward |
| Parry | Brannoc's forge trial | Brutes | Cindermaw, the King |
| Unison Burst | Ember Sigil | Emberridge on | the King, phase 3 |
| Double jump | Tidewalker Pearl | Mirrormere stones, old ledges | |
| Closing distance | Maressa's fight | spitter camps | Maressa |
| Perfect dodge, target switching | Stormreach stair | wraith pairs | Skarn |
| Skill Sigils | first level up: Maren points to the menu | | |
| Smith upgrades | Brannoc after the first camp | | |
| Evolution | Oona, when a Kith nears its level | | |

## 7. Strategy

**Elements, shrine by shrine.** Each shrine is weak to one element, and the
region before it should offer a Kith of that element, with a hint.

| Shrine | Its element | Bring | Where to find it before |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Roots | Verdant | Ember | starter, or the stray Cinderpup at the forge [built] |
| Cinders | Ember | Tide | starter, or the Emberridge hot springs [built] |
| Tides | Tide | Storm | Voltkit in the meadow [built] |
| Gales | Storm | Verdant | Mossling or Thornback, likely evolved by now [built] |
| Hollow Court | Gloam | your strongest | Gloam hits and is hit harder by everything [built] |

**Party roles.** Oona frames the third slot as: a hitter (Ember, Storm), a
protector (Bloom heals, Pearl Aegis shields, Stillwater freezes), and a counter
for whatever you are about to face. [words]

**Sword builds.** Skill Sigils already allow different styles; NPCs can name
them so players see the choice: [words]

- **Duellist** (Brannoc): Riposte, Blade Dancer. Parry and punish.
- **Dancer** (Skarn): Flash Step, Dash Slash, Second Wind. Dodge and dart.
- **Bonded** (Oona): Kith Tempo, Bond Fury, Elemental Edge. Let the Kith carry.

**Economy.** Motes buy blade, vitality and Dewdrop upgrades from Brannoc;
Threads (from Pip) buy new bonds; Sigils buy skills. [built] Pip's rumours make
the money point somewhere.

## 8. The world reacts

Small touches that make the story feel real as you progress:

- **Music grows.** The overworld music gains a layer each time a shrine wakes,
  until the ending plays the full song. [new] (in the music tables)
- **Colour returns.** Grey edges creep back from each woken region. [new]
- **Lanterns relight** on the Lantern Tree as shrines wake: one lit at the
  start, all nine at the end. [built]
- **People change their lines** after each chapter (Maren, Brannoc, Oona, Pip
  each get one new line per chapter). [words]
- **Husks remember.** Defeated Husks near a woken shrine sometimes leave a
  ghostly villager who says one line and fades. [new] optional

## 9. Telling the backstory

- **Echo Shards are memories.** [built] Each of the 16 plays a short scene
  from before the Hush when you pick it up. They are grouped by region, so each
  set tells one keeper's side, and within a region they always come back in
  story order, whichever shard you find first. Found memories can be read again
  under Memories in the pause menu.
  - Meadow (4): Maren teaching young Aurel and Lumen; the Choir's last
    festival; the Long Note; finding the baby in the roots.
  - Emberridge (3): Brannoc and Kiln at the forge; the night Brannoc ran; Kiln
    waiting.
  - Mirrormere (3): Maressa's song; Aurel asking her to stop singing; her
    choice.
  - Stormreach (3): Skarn and Aurel as boys; Skarn's oath; Skarn begging Aurel
    to take the crown off.
  - Gloamwood (3): Lumen going into the dark; Aurel at the edge of the
    Undersong; the crown.
  - All 16: a bonus memory, "Lullaby": Maren singing to the baby in the
    silence, the baby humming the last note back, and one lantern lighting.
    It plays straight after the 16th shard's own memory, whenever that is.
- **Boss lines.** Each keeper gets one line when the fight starts and one when
  freed. [words]
- **Hush Rifts** are where the Undersong leaks up. Sealing them is mopping up
  the King's damage. [built] [words]

## 10. Build order

Roughly from quickest and most valuable to largest.

1. **Words pass** [words]: rewrite Maren, Brannoc, Oona and Pip's lines per
   chapter, boss lines, shrine door inscriptions, element hints, the ending,
   and objectives that say *why* as well as *where*.
   **Done 2026-11-22.** All of it lives in `src/data.js` (`QUESTS`, `TALK`,
   `CHAT`, `DOORS`, `BOSS_INTRO`, `FREED`, `OPENS`). Each villager says their
   chapter lines once, then Brannoc, Pip and Oona open their menus and Maren
   repeats a short hint. The lanterns from step 5 came along too, so the
   dialogue about them is true. Lines about Sable are left out until Sable
   exists.
2. **Echo Shard memories** [words + small]: 16 short memories on pickup.
   **Done 2026-11-22.** The text is `MEMORIES` and `LULLABY` in `src/data.js`.
   Memories show in the dialogue box with their title and pale blue italics,
   and there is a Memories tab in the pause menu.
3. **Counter Kith and enemy introductions** [tweak]: a Cinderpup by the forge,
   Puddlefin in Emberridge, first Brute, Warder and Wraith on their own.
   **Done 2026-11-22.** All in `EXTRA_ZONES` and `SPRINGS` in `src/data.js`,
   kept apart from `CAMPS` and `KZONES` so the world (and every Echo Shard)
   stays where it was. The stray Cinderpup only appears once you have chosen a
   starter, and only if you own no Cinderpup. Villager lines can now carry a
   condition (`LINE_COND` in `src/main.js`), so Brannoc and Oona only mention
   things that are true for your save. Brutes, Warders and Wraiths each show a
   one-time tip the first time one notices you.
4. **Teaching moments** [new]: Brannoc's practice yard, Oona's guided Bond
   Rite, the forge parry trial.
   **Done 2026-11-22.** `src/trials.js` runs them; places and lines are
   `TRAINING` and `TRAIN_TALK` in `src/data.js`. Brannoc and Oona offer their
   training before their usual menu (with "I already know how" to skip it for
   good), and before the Shrine of Roots the objective suggests the yard, then
   Oona. Nothing is forced: the shrine is open either way. Rewards: 60 Motes
   (the yard, exactly a first blade hone), the Kith itself (Oona), and a Skill
   Sigil (the parry trial, from chapter 2). Walking away, falling or leaving
   the overworld stops a trial, and it can be started again.
5. **The world reacts** [new]: music layers, colour returning (lanterns done).
6. **Sable** [new]: three rival duels.
7. **The final phase and ending scene** [new]: Kith silenced, Lumen, the
   crown breaking.
8. **After the ending** [new small]: bond with Lumen, the bonus memory.

Steps 1 to 3 would already make the game feel like it has a story. Steps 4
and up are where it starts to feel crafted.
